Everyone's racing to give AI agents more autonomy.

I found a repo doing the opposite — and it has more stars than most of the "fully autonomous agent" projects.

It enforces obedience. It makes agents follow a deterministic plan and kills the steps they hallucinate along the way.

That matches everything I've seen running AI in production.

The bottleneck was never how smart the model is. It's how predictable it is.

An agent that's brilliant 90% of the time and quietly invents the other 10% isn't an employee. It's a liability you have to double-check — which means you've saved nothing.

The teams getting real leverage out of AI aren't the ones giving it the most freedom.

They're the ones who boxed it in until the output was boring and repeatable, then pointed it at the boring, repeatable work that was eating their week.

Autonomy is the demo.

Determinism is the product.

(The repo: babysitter — deterministic self-orchestration for agentic workflows.)
