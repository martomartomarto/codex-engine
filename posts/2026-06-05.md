Someone built a 3D engine in pure CSS. No WebGL, no canvas.

"3D on the web" almost always means reaching for Three.js — a heavy dependency most landing pages can't justify for one hero section.

polycss does it with nothing but matrix3d transforms. It renders polygon meshes straight in the DOM. A few hundred GitHub stars, shipped last week, barely anyone's seen it.

Why a marketer should care:

→ The sections people scroll past fastest are the flat ones. Depth and motion are what stop the thumb.
→ You don't need a render pipeline to add them. You need the right constraint.

The "wow" on a great landing page is almost never a budget problem. It's a craft problem someone already solved — for free.
