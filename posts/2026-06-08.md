Someone reverse-engineered Apple's animated video wallpapers and open-sourced the whole engine.

428 points on Hacker News in a day.

Nobody asked for this. No startup, no pricing page, no "book a demo." Just a person who looked at something polished, got curious about how it actually worked, and rebuilt it in the open.

I keep coming back to this kind of thing.

Because the gap between people who use tools and people who take them apart is the same gap I see in growth.

Most teams consume the dashboard. A few ask what's actually behind the number, then go rebuild the thing that generates it.

The first group ships reports.

The second group ships systems.

Curiosity about how things actually work isn't a hobby. It's the whole edge.

(The repo: phosphene — a video wallpaper engine for macOS, reverse-engineered from Apple's.)
