I scanned every product that launched today. The copy tells on itself.

Nearly every tagline led with the technology — "AI-powered," "agentic," "LLM-native." Almost none led with the job it does for you.

Here's the test I run on launch copy:

Find-and-replace "AI" with nothing. Read it again.

→ If it still describes a clear outcome — you launched a product.
→ If the sentence falls apart — you launched a feature and called it a company.

The tools that survive past launch week aren't the ones with the best model. They're the ones whose copy still means something after you delete the buzzword.
